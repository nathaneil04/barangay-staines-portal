Explore available national and local social welfare programs, eligibility guidelines, and master lists of registered beneficiaries in Barangay Santa Ines.

Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps)
DSWD / National Program

Conditional cash transfer grant aimed at improving health, nutrition, and education for eligible low-income households.

Requirements:
  • Listahanan 3 Assessment Eligibility
  • Barangay Certificate of Indigency
  • Valid ID & Household Member Enrollment Proof
🏥
AICS Financial Assistance
DSWD / Barangay Endorsed

Emergency support providing medical, hospitalization, burial, or transportation aid for residents facing crisis situations.

Requirements:
  • Certificate of Indigency (for AICS purpose)
  • Medical Certificate / Billing Statement / Death Certificate
  • Valid Government-issued ID of Claimant
👵
Indigent Senior Citizen Social Pension
NCSC / OSCA / DSWD

Monthly stipend allocated to qualifying indigent senior citizens aged 60 and above with no regular income or pension.

Requirements:
  • Senior Citizen ID (OSCA Card)
  • Barangay Certification (Proof of Indigency/Residency)
  • Birth Certificate or Marriage Contract
🛠️
TUPAD Program
DOLE / Community Work

Community-based safety net scheme providing short-term emergency wage employment for displaced or seasonal workers.

Requirements:
  • Barangay Clearance or Residency Certificate
  • 1x2 Photo & Government ID
  • Underemployed / Displaced Status Verification
👶
Solo Parent Benefits & ID Application
MSWDO / LGU Paniqui

Comprehensive welfare services, discounts, and potential financial subsidies for qualified solo parents.

Requirements:
  • Solo Parent Assessment Form
  • Barangay Certification of Single Parent Status
  • Birth Certificate(s) of Dependent(s)
♿
PWD Welfare & Medical Assistance
PDAO / MSWDO

Registration, discount privileges, assistive device distribution, and medical aid enrollment for PWD residents.

Requirements:
  • Medical Certificate detailing Disability / Condition
  • Barangay Certificate of Residency
  • 1x1 ID Pictures & Valid ID
